**The World in One Page — 17 November 2026.** A single 1024×1024 broadsheet front page that sums up one very crowded news day. The lead illustration shows Ukraine's strike on the Kerch Bridge to Crimea, its road span dropping into the sea under dusk light, with Russia's missile answer on Kyiv underneath. A "Day in Numbers" strip carries the figures that defined the date — 40 Amazon Leo satellites lost with the failed ULA Vulcan, $4.8T of unverified Pentagon assets in a ninth straight failed audit, Brent at $92.76, eight West Bank firms sanctioned, a third US carrier in the Middle East. Six illustrated cards cover Trump's ASEAN arrival in Manila, the mined tankers in the Strait of Hormuz, JPMorgan's software-bug outage, Nvidia's first earnings since Jensen Huang stepped down, the Vulcan failure, and the UK sanctions, while an "Also Today" strip picks up Japan's scrambled jets, the Houthi advance in Yemen, and Gérard Depardieu's guilty plea. The entry program itself is deliberately boring: it copies the shipped `artwork.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte-for-byte and refuses to publish anything whose size, dimensions, or pinned SHA-256 do not match.
